How to play DOM-ination

Start it

  1. Run python3 -m http.server 8000 in /home/py/system32, then open http://localhost:8000.
  2. Skip the boot screen with any key, click or press a key on the lock screen, then click Sign in.
  3. Double-click free_prizes.html on the desktop. It opens in Edgy.
  4. Click the big green CLAIM YOUR FREE GPU button to begin the 60-second run.

If you see "Maximize the window to play", the Edgy window is too small. The game needs at least 760×480, so double-click the title bar to maximize it.

The goal

Pop-up ads keep appearing. Survive 60 seconds without 24 or more open at once. The only thing you do is click to close them.

The screen

The bar across the top shows, left to right:

  • the countdown
  • the clutter meter (open pop-ups out of 24), which turns yellow at 60%, red at 80%, and shakes at 90%
  • how many you've closed
  • the wave (1/3 to 3/3)
  • a joke counter of how many <body> nodes the page currently has

The key rule: pop-ups multiply

Every pop-up has a 5-second life. From 3.5 s its border flashes red and a thin bar at the bottom shrinks. At 5 s it spawns a copy and starts over. Close the flashing ones first. The timer bar shows what's about to multiply.

Pop-up types

Type What to do
Classic Click the ✕ in its title bar.
Drifter The same, but it slides around and bounces off the edges. Click the ✕ as it moves.
Fake-X Don't click the big red ✕. It's bait. Click the tiny grey "No thanks, I hate free stuff" link in the body.
Runaway It hops away when your cursor gets within about 70 px. After 3 hops its title changes to "fine." and it stops. Wait for that, or catch it as it lands.
Chain Closing it spawns two small "Are you sure?" pop-ups. Click the ✕ on each.
Update boss Appears once, at 50 s. Its ✕ jumps to a new corner after every click, and it takes 5 clicks. It counts as 3 toward clutter.

Bait buttons. From wave 2, some pop-ups carry a big colourful CLAIM button. Never click it. Any bait click, including the Fake-X's big ✕, spawns 2 more pop-ups.

Waves

  • Wave 1 (0–20 s): Classic and Drifter only. Spawns are slow, so learn the flow.
  • Wave 2 (20–40 s): Fake-X and Runaway join, and CLAIM buttons start appearing.
  • Wave 3 (40–60 s): Chain pop-ups join, spawns speed up, and the boss arrives at 50 s.

A banner announces each wave. Spawns pause for 1.5 s while it shows.

Tips

  1. Triage, don't sweep. Close whatever is closest to flashing, not the nearest pop-up.
  2. Aim for the right spot. On a Fake-X, go straight for the tiny link. Don't hover near the big ✕.
  3. Keep your cursor still near a Runaway. Let it use up its 3 hops, then close it.
  4. Stay below about 60% clutter (the meter stays green) so one bad moment can't tip you over.
  5. Clicking a pop-up's body raises it above others, which helps when they overlap.
  6. Keyboard works too. Tab to a ✕ and press Enter.

Pausing

The game pauses automatically when you minimize the window or click the desktop outside it (a "Paused" overlay appears). Click the overlay or the window to resume. No time is lost or gained.

Winning and losing

  • Win: reaching 0:00 with fewer than 24 open. Everything shrinks into the centre and a "You won: popup_guard.sys" card shows your stats. You must click Install to repair the component. Closing the window without installing doesn't count.
  • Lose: 24 open at any moment. Click "Try again". Losing doesn't change anything.

After a win, the shell shows "popup_guard.sys restored", the desktop icon gets a green check, and the fake ad pop-ups on the desktop stop. If you replay, the start screen shows your best closed count.

Secrets (spoilers, skip if you like)

  • Boss in under 2 s: close it within 2 s of its appearing for a surprise last word.
  • Gullible: click 5 bait buttons in one run.
  • Bold strategy: close nothing for the first 10 s, and survive wave 1.

The idea

Enemy cards fall down the arena. You hide them by typing a CSS selector and pressing Enter. The game builds this rule from what you type:

:where(#arena) :is(YOUR SELECTOR) { display: none; }
  • Any enemy your selector matches disappears.
  • Don't hide the allies, which are the <p> cards. Each one you hide costs a ♥. You start with 3.
  • An enemy that reaches the bottom also costs a ♥.
  • Your rules stay active in player.css (right panel) until you delete them with ✕. There are 6 slots, and a 7th rule evicts the oldest.

The selector cheat sheet

Type this It matches
.red anything with class="red"
#boss-3f2a the element with that id
div every <div>
div.enemy a div that also has class enemy

Specificity is a triple of (ids, classes, tags). #a.b.c div is (1,2,1). Compare left to right, and the first bigger number wins.

Ties go to you, because player.css loads after the enemies' stylesheet. Inline style="" beats any selector, and only !important beats inline.

As you type, a preview line tells you what you would hit.

How to win Strata

Winning means beating the Garbage Collector in the Kernel. These are the steps in order.

1. First day: tools and shelter

  1. Punch a tree for logs. Put a log in the crafting grid to get 4 planks.
  2. Put 2 planks on top of each other to get 4 sticks. Put 4 planks in the 2×2 grid to make a crafting table.
  3. Right-click the table to get the 3×3 grid.
  4. Wooden pickaxe: 3 planks across the top, then 2 sticks down the middle.
  5. Mine stone and craft a stone pickaxe the same way with cobblestone. A furnace is 8 cobblestone in a ring.
  6. Mine coal. Coal over a stick gives 4 torches. Place them with right-click, on the floor or on a wall.
  7. Before night, build a small shelter. Monsters spawn in the dark, so use torches.
  8. Bed: 3 wool over 3 planks. Get wool from sheep. Right-click the bed at night to skip to morning and set your respawn point.
  9. Food: kill animals and cook the raw meat in the furnace. Hold right-click on food for 1.6 s to eat. You can't sprint when hunger is 6 or less.

2. Iron age

  1. Iron ore needs a stone pickaxe. Smelt raw iron in the furnace with coal (4 s each).
  2. Make an iron pickaxe, iron sword, and iron armor (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, in the usual shapes).
  3. Craft a bow: 3 sticks and 3 string in a column pattern. Craft arrows: flint over stick over feather gives 4.
  4. Get string from Creepcrawlers, feathers from chickens, and flint from gravel (10% drop).

3. Cobalt

  1. Dig deep. Cobalt ore is at y 2 to 16 and needs an iron pickaxe.
  2. Smelt raw cobalt in the furnace (8 s each) into cobalt ingots.
  3. Make a cobalt pickaxe (3 ingots over 2 sticks), a cobalt sword, and cobalt armor (80% damage reduction).
  4. Craft a Tracer: 8 iron ingots in a ring around 1 cobalt ingot. Hold it and the compass strip at the top points to your target.

  3. Stand in the Rift for 1 s and you fall into the Underlayer. You arrive on a small platform with a return Rift.
  4. Bring torches, food, arrows, spare blocks, and your best armor. It's dark, with lava, Daemons (fireballs) and Glitches (they teleport next to you and rewrite blocks).
  5. If you die here you respawn on the surface. Your items wait where you died for 10 minutes.

5. Raid three vaults (you need 3 Kernel Shards)

  1. Holding the Tracer, walk to the nearest vault. It's a tall glowing tower with a doorway on its north side.
  2. Break the mob spawner in the hall with a pickaxe (about 5 s). Daemons stop appearing, and the door across the stairs grinds open.
  3. Climb the stairs to the lava parkour room. Jump between the rack platforms. Lava does about 8 HP per second.
  4. Take the second staircase up to the shard room and fight the Vault Warden (80 HP). Every 10 s it raises its shield for 3 s, and only hits from behind count then. Circle round it.
